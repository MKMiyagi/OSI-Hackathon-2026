# Pitch outline (7 slides) — DRAFT for T2

All MAEs are development-set (val, reused) numbers unless a slide says otherwise; nested-CV numbers are outer-fold means on pooled train+val, not the quarantined test set. Target is `hv_chen`, not measured hardness. Figures are in `wiki/figures/` (light and `_dark` variants).

1. **Problem and framing:** Hardness needs DFT moduli, so can a short composition-only formula emulate the DFT-derived Chen proxy `hv_chen`, and how close does it get to a Magpie-SVR black box (not "beating Chen", not measured hardness)? Figure: none (text slide; optionally `t1_routes.png` as a teaser of the d_023 effect).
2. **Frozen protocol and quarantine:** Element-set-grouped 70/15/15 split frozen first, test file kept outside the repo with only its hash committed, fixed scorer, 3 seeds per cycle against a stored 9-seed reference. Figure: none (diagram or text slide).
3. **What formulas reach vs the black box:** The best Magpie-only formulas close about half of the constant-to-SVR gap (48% at c<=12), d_023 formulas about 62%, and the best is 1.27x the SVR's MAE (MASE); 5.04 constant, 2.82 SVR. Figures: `svr_f4_pareto_blackbox.png` (val envelopes and nested-CV markers against the constant, the SVR and the Mazhnik-Oganov oracle that needs true B,G) and `svr_f5_gap_closed.png` (gap closed, 62% annotated); `metrics_mase_vs_complexity.png` (gap and MASE vs complexity) as the earlier val-only version. Backup behind this slide: `svr_f1_moduli_benchmark.png` and `svr_f2_parity_moduli.png` (SVR B/G errors vs Tehrani 2018's published RMSE, different protocol), `svr_f7_error_propagation.png` (Hv inherits the moduli errors).
4. **The agent loop and two reviewer catches:** One hypothesis per cycle with a Codex review each time; the reviewer caught (a) the saturated c<=9 metric (9 seeds, same equation, sd 0.0001, so we moved to a c<=12 multi-seed headline) and (b) the c018 band-gap term (the 3.77 formula subtracted eV from dimensionless counts, 94.7% of its gain came from 10 val rows, and removing the term gave back essentially all of the gain). Figures: `descriptor_cycles.png` (cycles 003-018 only; no descriptor cleared the bar) and `mae_vs_complexity.png` (annotated c018_s0 point, saturated low-complexity front).
5. **The d_023 formula, its reading, and robustness:** `d_023 / (NdValence + 2.376)` (cohesive-energy density over a d-electron term, a post-hoc stiffness reading) lowers the Pareto front, and under nested grouped CV it is selected and beats Magpie-only in 5/5 folds (c<=12: 3.61 vs 3.79, SVR 2.81), with the caveat that d_023 was screened on train and scored once on val before adoption. Figures: `t1_routes.png` (whole front moves down), `svr_f5_gap_closed.png` (val and nested-CV gap closed on one plot) and `svr_f4_pareto_blackbox.png` (nested fold means at c<=9/12/15 next to the val fronts, SVR bar).
6. **Where it fails and the shear-proxy coverage finding:** Oxides are overpredicted (bias +2.6 GPa, worse than the constant), ZrB2 and BaC2 share a composition fraction but sit 19 GPa below and 15 GPa above the formula, the tail (>= 20 GPa) is underpredicted, and the shear/Pugh fix is blocked because elemental G is missing for B, C, N, O, Si, P, S and others. Figures: `svr_f3_parity_hv.png` (SVR vs c8 vs c5 parity with per-family MAE tables; oxides overpredicted by the formulas), `svr_f6_family_mae.png` (per-family bars), `metrics_scatter_best_c9.png` (tail underprediction) and `metrics_binned_mae.png` (binned error).
