## Findings
1. **No new descriptor moved d_023.** 030 (B/C/N fraction) not selected; 031 (metal fraction) selected 3/3 with the registered negative sign but -0.023 at c<=12 (reviewer: gain not established; d_031 appears only at c>=10 and in one seed's c15 winner); 032 did not recover the 031 gain (d_031 1/3 seeds, d_027 3/3, same c11 as cycle 021). d_031 is also the only descriptor that survived the "majority of seeds" precondition, so it was the one extended into 032 and the nested CV.
2. **Shear/Pugh hypothesis: untestable as specified.** pymatgen and the handbooks (CRC, Kaye & Laby via the Wikipedia data page) have no elemental shear modulus for B, C, N, O, F, Si, P, S, As, Ge, Ga, In, Cl, Br, I (and no bulk modulus for N, O, F, In, Tc, Sr), so no cited fallback exists for the elements that dominate the hard and oxide rows. Row-drop removes 59-68% of train/val rows, 63 of 86 train and 12-13 of 17 val rows >= 20 GPa, and score.py cannot score NaN features. Supplement on the 119 covered val rows (equal training rows, own scorer): neither d_030 nor d_028 adds a consistent gain over d_023 (+0.013 / -0.045 at c<=12, -0.033 / -0.051 at c<=15, all < 0.08 and < the seed spread of the d_023-only arm). On this subset d_023 alone already closes 85% of the constant-to-SVR gap. The oxide/boride/carbide/nitride question remains untested; it needs G values for B, C, N, O, Si, ... from a source the team will defend.
3. **Nested grouped CV (033): the d_023 gain survives repartitioning of the pooled data, but it is smaller than on val and the descriptor is not independently validated.** Fold-mean test MAE at c<=9/12/15: Magpie 4.039/3.787/3.767, +d_023 3.618/3.608/3.493, SVR 2.810 (fit-row-matched 2.952), constant 4.889. d_023 selected in 5/5 folds at every window, better than Magpie-only in 5/5 folds in every window (paired mean gain 0.42/0.18/0.27 GPa; on val it was 0.50/0.30/0.28), and the c8 form is the c<=9 pick in 4 of 5 folds. Gap closed at c<=12: 62% (53% Magpie only); MASE vs SVR 1.29; pooled tail (>= 20 GPa, n = 103) 10.07 vs 8.74 SVR vs 12.84 Magpie. Caveats (Codex, ACCEPT WITH FIXES): d_023 was screened on train labels and scored once on val before adoption, and train+val is the pooled set, so "never seen" holds for the equation search but not for the descriptor choice; one PySR fit per cell; folds share ~3/4 of training rows; the SVR uses 132 features and separate log B/log G supervision. d_031 shows no consistent benefit under this protocol (-0.007 at c<=12, +0.025 at c<=15; selected in 3/5 folds).
4. **L1 loss (034): no consistent improvement of the [5,15) GPa bin at equal complexity.** c8 is the identical equation (bin MAE 3.278, the constant's is 3.310); L1 is better at c5 (3.80 vs 4.00), worse at c11/c14/c15, a few hundredths either way at c7/c10/c13. Its c<=12 headline gain (-0.040 vs cycle 020, below 1 reference sd) comes from a c11 equation that improves the <5 and >=25 bins and worsens [5,15). The stored reference is L2, so no L1 reference arm exists (SPLIT_FREEZE.md asks for one if L1 is pursued).
5. **Where d_023 still fails (step 0):** oxides (bias +2.6, worse than the constant), within-family discrimination of covalent vs ionic compounds (ZrB2 and TaN underpredicted by 15-19 GPa while BaC2 and CoN are overpredicted by 10-15), and intermetallic ranking. A single scalar fraction (d_007, d_031) cannot separate rows that share it (Codex: ZrB2 and BaC2 both have B/C/N fraction 2/3; TaN and CoN both 1/2).
6. **Best val numbers of the night are single-seed and non-new:** c030_s0 c14 3.5632 (no new descriptor) and c031_s2 c15 3.5752 (d_031), both below the c<=15 d_023-alone mean (3.6514) by ~0.1 but within search scatter of the 15-complexity front; not recommended as candidates.
