Every panel title carries "development set (val, reused)" or "nested CV (train+val folds)". No other metric is computed.

Published numbers on the figures: Tehrani 2018 10-fold-CV RMSE (B 17.2, G 16.5 GPa), r^2 (0.94, 0.84), n = 2572, 14 structural descriptors (verification table rows 2, 3, 5, 6 in `wiki/COMPARISON_TO_LITERATURE.md`); Chen 2011 Eq. 11 (row 7); Mazhnik-Oganov 2019 coefficients (row 9, newly verified 2026-10-22, `wiki/CITATION_AUDIT_COMPARISON.md`). **Omitted:** a Tian 2012 oracle line, because its coefficients (row 10) could not be verified at source; the F4 caption says so. ## Choices a reader should know about
- **Colours:** five family hues do not pass the all-pairs colour-vision validator of the dataviz palette (worst pairs fail the normal-vision floor), so family is also encoded by marker shape on every scatter/table; this is stated in the captions.
- **F1 CIs:** the SVR rows reproduce `ledger/bg_errors.csv`; the symbolic rows are re-evaluated from the stored T1 equations with the same 2000-draw element-set-group bootstrap; the 3-seed symbolic bar bootstraps the mean of the seeds' RMSEs. The paper's folds are described as 10-fold CV with no mention of grouping; the caption says "fold grouping not described", not "ungrouped".
- **F4/F5 val curves** are best-so-far envelopes of val MAE (selected on val, optimistic); nested markers use different rows (2120 pooled vs 374) and different constants, so levels are only roughly comparable; F5 states that agreement is not independent corroboration.
- **F6 Magpie-only bar** = per seed, the best-val equation at c<=12 (the CLAUDE.md headline selection), mean of 9 seeds with range whiskers (recomputed MAEs match the ledger; overall mean 3.98). c5 and c8 are single c020_s0 equations, not seed averages.
- **F7 panel C** is a delta-method propagation through Chen's equation (an algebraic consequence, not a fit); no correlation or other statistic is computed.
- The oracle MAE (3.126) is our computation from the verified Mazhnik-Oganov formula, reproduced in the script and equal to `wiki/BASELINES.md`; it is not a physical-accuracy bound.
