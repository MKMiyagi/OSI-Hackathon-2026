## Verdict (revised after reviewer, codex regular tier)
Recurrent compact candidate; no established improvement. In the 3 of 5 seeds that find it, d_008 yields the same c8 equation (val 4.0300 after rounding), 0.165 GPa below the Magpie-only c9 equation; over all 5 seeds the mean gain is 0.099 GPa. Recurrence shows search reproducibility, not independent predictive validation. The effect is confined to c<=9: at c<=12 the difference is +0.03 (wrong sign, ~0) and at c<=15 -0.028, both within noise. Because seeds with/without are not matched searches (`deterministic=False`), the paired SDs/win counts above depend on arbitrary pairing; the unpaired Welch view is the safer one (mean diff, approx. 95% CI, n=5 vs 5): c<=9 -0.099 [-0.211, +0.013]; c<=12 +0.030 [-0.064, +0.124]; c<=15 -0.028 [-0.074, +0.018]. All CIs include 0. So: d_008 offers a more compact route to ~4.03 GPa, not a higher ceiling; real-vs-noise is unresolved at this n, leaning "weak, plausible signal".
Physics/units: H/GPa = N_unfilled + 381.72 exp(-(d_008 + Row)): chi and Row are dimensionless, N_unfilled is a count, so GPa units are implicit in the fitted constants (equal unit decay rates for chi and Row, and the additive unfilled-count term, are fit outcomes, not derived laws). Composition cannot separate polymorphs. Everything here is exploratory: equations, descriptors and windows are all chosen on the same val set; target is Chen-derived. `sr/ablation_report.py` "distinct structures" counts masked textual forms, not algebraic equivalence (the 5 without-arm forms are algebraically one function).
