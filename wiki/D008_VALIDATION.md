## Verdict (revised after reviewer, codex regular tier)
Recurrent compact candidate; no established improvement. In the 3 of 5 seeds that find it, d_008 yields the same c8 equation (val 4.0300 after rounding), 0.165 GPa below the Magpie-only c9 equation; over all 5 seeds the mean gain is 0.099 GPa. Recurrence shows search reproducibility, not independent predictive validation. The effect is confined to c<=9: at c<=12 the difference is +0.03 (wrong sign, ~0) and at c<=15 -0.028, both within noise. Because seeds with/without are not matched searches (`deterministic=False`), the paired SDs/win counts above depend on arbitrary pairing; the unpaired Welch view is the safer one (mean diff, approx. 95% CI, n=5 vs 5): c<=9 -0.099 [-0.211, +0.013]; c<=12 +0.030 [-0.064, +0.124]; c<=15 -0.028 [-0.074, +0.018]. All CIs include 0. So: d_008 offers a more compact route to ~4.03 GPa, not a better result than the best observed under this search configuration; real-vs-noise is unresolved at this n, leaning "weak, plausible signal".
Physics/units: H/GPa = N_unfilled + 381.72 exp(-(d_008 + Row)): chi and Row are dimensionless, N_unfilled is a count, so GPa units are implicit in the fitted constants (equal unit decay rates for chi and Row, and the additive unfilled-count term, are fit outcomes, not derived laws). Composition cannot separate polymorphs. Everything here is exploratory: equations, descriptors and windows are all chosen on the same val set; target is Chen-derived. `sr/ablation_report.py` "distinct structures" counts masked textual forms, not algebraic equivalence (the 5 without-arm forms are algebraically one function).

## Phase 3 — longer search (niterations 120000, seed 0, one fit per arm, run in parallel, procs 4 each)
`long_w_s0` (with d_008; 3604 s, hit the 3600 s timeout at 99.8% of iterations) and `long_wo_s0` (without; 3483 s, 99.97%). Both effectively completed.
Best val_mae by window (with / without): c<=6 4.4229/4.4230; c<=9 4.0300/4.1946; c<=12 4.0300/3.9771; c<=15 3.8923/3.8923.
- Selection: the with-arm again picks `m_NUnfilled + 381.72/exp(d_008 + m_Row)` at c8 (val 4.0300) — the same equation, at the same minimum complexity, as the 22000-iteration runs. The deeper search did NOT select d_008 earlier (no lower-complexity use) and did not find better compact equations: c<=9 and c<=15 results are unchanged from the 22k seeds, and the without arm's c9 winner is still the Magpie equation (4.1946).
- The without-arm c12 equation (3.9771: `NUnfilled + ((NsValence/0.4518 - NpValence)/(exp(Row)/MendeleevNumber))`) again beats the with-arm's best c<=12, matching the 22k finding that d_008 gives no c<=12 advantage. c<=15 is identical (3.8923) in both arms: both converge on the same Magpie-only c15 equation.
- Conclusion: search depth is not the limiting factor; the 22k search was already converged on this front for seed 0. n=1 per arm, so this says nothing about seed variance; it supports "the c8 d_008 equation is a stable optimum, not a search-shortfall artefact".
